The real problem

Day 1 of your plan feels exciting. Day 9 is when it gets quiet.

  • You rewrote your pitch three times and still can’t tell if it’s good.
  • Your portfolio looks “okay,” but okay doesn’t get replies.
  • You skipped two days, then four, and now the workbook is just sitting there.

That doesn’t mean you lack talent. It means no one is looking at your work yet.

Coaching changes that.

Who it’s for

Coaching works when you bring your actual work.

This is for you if

  • You finished the challenge and want a real coach to look at your actual work
  • You started your 30-day plan but keep getting stuck or skipping days
  • You’re not sure your pitch or portfolio sounds right to a US client
  • You want to practice interviews before the real one

This is not for you if

  • You want someone to find you a client or a job
  • You’re looking for shortcuts instead of practice
  • You’re not ready to do the work between sessions
